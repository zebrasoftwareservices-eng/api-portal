## Technical design (tech-lead, 2026-11-11)
Each subscription is an app on a plan (`apps.plan_id`), and Kong enforces a plan with one consumer-scoped `rate-limiting` plugin per subscription that holds the rate limit and a calendar-month (UTC) quota, recorded in ADR 0009 with `cluster` as the default policy (`PORTAL_RATE_LIMIT_POLICY=local` for operators who accept per-node counts). The contract adds public product endpoints, admin product and plan endpoints, `planId` on `POST /v1/apps`, change plan, cancel (`DELETE /v1/apps/{appId}`) and key regenerate, each with Kong compensation so a failed gateway call leaves nothing half-done. Existing apps migrate onto a built-in `Unlimited` plan, and APIs in a product can no longer be reached through the spec 004 per-API flow. Sixteen tasks are in the spec; Redis policy and moving legacy apps onto plans are recorded as follow-ups.
