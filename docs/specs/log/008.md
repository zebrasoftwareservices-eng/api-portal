
## Product manager — 2026-11-10
Wrote spec 008 (teams and roles) from JSO-236 as a draft with nine Given/When/Then criteria: personal teams and migration of existing apps, creating and switching teams, email invites with Admin, Member and Read-only roles, and keys that survive removing a member or deleting an account. Failure paths cover Read-only and Member 403s, invalid invitations and the last-Admin guard. Team deletion and renaming, staff roles (015) and per-team plans (009, 010) are out of scope. Six open questions wait on Elango, the tech lead and the UI designer, chiefly what happens when the last Admin deletes their account.

## Tech lead — 2026-11-10
Designed spec 008 and recorded ADR 0008: teams are portal tables (`teams`, `team_members`, `team_invitations`) rather than Better Auth's organization plugin, each app belongs to one team, and Kong consumers stay one per app, so inviting, removing and role changes never call Kong and the upgrade migration needs no Kong re-tagging. The contract adds `/v1/teams`, members, invitations (preview and accept by token in the body), `PUT /v1/me/current-team`, `teamId` and `role` on apps, and the 403 `team-role-forbidden` and 409 `team-needs-admin` problems. The open questions are designed on the recommended answers: the last Admin can't delete their account while others remain, a sole-member team's apps go with the account, invitations last 7 days, accepting verifies the email, and duplicate invites get a 409. One new rule needs Elango's eye: a personal team takes no invitations, so deleting an account never removes apps someone else uses.

## UI designer — 2026-11-10
Wrote docs/design/008-teams-and-roles.md: a team switcher above the `h1` on every My apps page, a create-team page, a team page (invite, members, pending invitations, leave), change-role, remove and leave dialogs, and the `/invitations/accept` page with a view for every preview state and the wrong-account case. Controls a role can't use are hidden with one line saying who can, Read-only sees key metadata without New key or Revoke, and non-members always get "We can't find this…" to match the server's 404. The 007 delete-account dialog now names sole-member teams that go and shared teams that stay, and shows `team-needs-admin` with links to the teams; copy for the invitation email and all new problem types is included. No new tokens; sign-up and sign-in gain an optional `email` query parameter to prefill the invited address.
